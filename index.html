How it works

Three steps. No sales call.

01 · about two minutes

Tell us the workload

The model, the dataset size and the GPU hours you expect. In your own words, or on the console.

$ vajra quote --model 70b --hours 24h →
02 · within one working day

Get a plan and a firm quote

A capacity plan and a price in rupees, from our team in Nepal. No sales call, no back-and-forth.

$ plan.pdf · firm NPR price · reserved capacity
03 · billed by the second

Deploy and start sharing

We stand up the systems on your GPUs (or a partner's). You start sharing cards, routing models and running notebooks — the meter runs only while your jobs run.

$ vajra deploy --on your-fleet --sharing on --api claude
